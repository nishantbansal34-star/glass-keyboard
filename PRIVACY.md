# Glass Keys — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 14 October 2026_

Glass Keys is a keyboard app made by Nishant Bansal (Aligarh, India). This policy explains what the app does with your information.

## The short version
**Everything you type stays on your phone.** Glass Keys has no internet permission, so it cannot send your typing, clipboard, or any other data anywhere. There are no ads, no analytics and no accounts.

## What the app stores on your phone
To work well, Glass Keys keeps the following **only in the app's private storage on your device**:
- **Words and phrases you type** (so suggestions, autocorrect and swipe typing learn your style). Not stored when you're in a password field, when an app asks for no personalised learning, or when Incognito is on.
- **Clipboard history** you copy while the keyboard is in use (pinned items until you delete them; others are trimmed automatically). Not saved in Incognito.
- **Quick texts, price list, business details and orders** that you enter yourself.
- **Settings and theme choices.**

You can delete learned words at any time (Settings → Your typing / "Forget all learned words"), clear the clipboard, or uninstall the app to remove everything.

## Permissions
- **Microphone** (optional): only when you tap the mic for voice typing. Speech is processed by your phone's speech service; Glass Keys does not record or keep audio.
- **Contacts** (optional, off by default): only if you turn on "Suggest names from my contacts". Names are read on the phone to suggest them while typing; they are never copied off the device.
- **Vibration**: for key-press feedback.

## Backups
"Backup & restore" writes a file **only where you choose to save it**. The app never uploads it.

## Children
Glass Keys is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any data from anyone.
